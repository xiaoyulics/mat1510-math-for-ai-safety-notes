## Cross-checks beyond single entries

- Labels vs entries: every `[Authors(Year)]` label agrees with the author count and year of its entry (two authors "A and B", three or more "A et~al."; years match the arXiv v1, publication or post year).
- Label uniqueness: in references.tex (169 items) no label occurs twice, and the lettered sets are complete: Anthropic(2026a-d), DeepSeek-AI(2025a-b), OpenAI(2026a-f), Betley et~al.(2025a-b). The letters are assigned by hand and follow neither title order nor date (for example DeepSeek-AI(2025a) is the December V3.2 report and (2025b) the January R1 paper). This is harmless because the labels are written by hand; no action unless you want BibTeX-style lettering.
- Fragments vs references.tex: all 55 keys of L05 and L06 are in references.tex with identical text, except `sharma2025`, which tools/refs_override.tex replaces by a longer author list (correct). The L07 and L08 fragments letter their OpenAI items differently (`openai2026dns` 2026d, `openai2026math` 2026e) from references.tex (2026e, 2026f); the override file already re-letters them, so `openai2026spec` as OpenAI(2026d) is consistent.
- Claim spot checks (not a claim audit): `willison2025` supported, `wani2026` supported, `kramar2026` consistent (its abstract says the findings informed deployment of misuse-mitigation probes in user-facing Gemini), `anthropicdocs2026` not supported by the cited page (see row), `openai2024o1` not checkable here.

## Method and limits

Only WebFetch and WebSearch were used. Page text from WebFetch is produced by a small summarizing model; where a detail was decisive I confirmed it a second way (a second fetch, Crossref, or the first page of the PDF viewed directly). The fetch tool itself cached some PDFs in the session's tool-results folder; scratch scripts for the label and diff checks are in the scratchpad folder bibcheckA. dblp and OpenReview refused automated access (bot-protection pages, not bypassed), so ICLR venues of `loshchilov2019` and `holtzman2020` rest on the authors' arXiv comments and the others on the iclr.cc pages or the PDF headers.
